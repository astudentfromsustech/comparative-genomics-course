## Answer

A dN/dS result licenses a deliberately narrow statement: **under the fitted codon model, the data reject a specified null in favour of an excess of nonsynonymous substitution relative to synonymous substitution in the tested scope**. A gene-wide \(\omega>1\) supports historical diversifying selection averaged across that gene/branch; it does not establish adaptation of the whole gene, a phenotype, a causal amino-acid change, or the time/agent of selection. A branch-site LRT supports \(\omega>1\) at *some unobserved codon class* on *predeclared foreground branch(es)*, not every foreground codon or branch; BEB site calls are conditional localization estimates, not independent confirmations. [Yang & Nielsen 2002](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/19/6/908/1094851)

This conditional wording matters because the standard models equate synonymous substitutions with a neutral baseline and assume a correct codon alignment, gene tree, no recombination within the fitted partition, single-nucleotide instantaneous changes, and an adequate stationary/reversible mutation model. Violations can produce an excellent likelihood-ratio statistic without adaptive amino-acid evolution. In particular, recombination can drive nominal 5% site-test type-I error towards 90%, gBGC was estimated to explain >20% of elevated-dN/dS primate candidates in one scan, and multinucleotide mutations can generate branch-site positives as often as the empirical test does under realistic simulated rates. [Anisimova, Nielsen & Yang 2003](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1462615/) [Ratnakumar et al. 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2935097/) [Venkat, Hahn & Thornton 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-018-0584-5)

Accordingly, the publishable claim is usually “candidate episodic diversifying selection, robust to stated alignment/tree/recombination/composition and multiplicity checks,” plus orthogonal biological evidence—not “proved positive selection.” Relaxed constraint and reduced \(N_e\) commonly elevate \(\omega\) without \(\omega>1\); test those explicitly with RELAX and population-genetic/functional context. [Wertheim et al. 2015](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/32/3/820/981440) [Eyre-Walker et al. 2002](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/19/12/2142/997518)

## Key findings

- **Model family and hypotheses.** NG86 is a counting approximation whose authors cautioned that it underestimates large numbers of nonsynonymous changes; GY94 is the likelihood codon-Markov framework that accommodates transition/transversion and codon-frequency bias. Neither raw pairwise \(\omega\) estimate is itself a significance test. [Nei & Gojobori 1986](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.molbev.a040410) [Goldman & Yang 1994](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/11/5/725/1008711)

- **PAML site/branch models.** M1a/M2a asks whether a model with a pervasive \(\omega>1\) site class fits better than nearly-neutral site classes; M7/M8 asks the analogous question after modeling background site heterogeneity with a beta distribution. Branch models test whether preassigned branches have different *mean* \(\omega\), which is not automatically a test of positive selection; branch-site Model A tests \(\omega_2>1\) only on preassigned foreground branch(es). [Nielsen & Yang 1998](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1460041/) [Zhang, Nielsen & Yang 2005](https://websites.umich.edu/~zhanglab/publications/2005/Zhang_2005_MBE_22_2472.pdf)

- **HyPhy’s hypotheses are more sharply scoped.** BUSTED tests gene-wide episodic diversification—at least one site on at least one test branch—not site-specific selection; aBSREL tests individual branches while adapting branch-model complexity; MEME tests individual codons for episodic/persistent selection; RELAX tests whether a priori test branches’ \(\omega\) distribution contracts toward (\(K<1\)) or moves away from (\(K>1\)) neutrality relative to reference branches. [BUSTED documentation](https://hyphy.org/methods/selection-methods/) [Smith et al. 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4408413/) [Murrell et al. 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3395634/) [Wertheim et al. 2015](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/32/3/820/981440)

- **Paralogy is not a minor preprocessing defect.** Mixed paralogs make columns and branch labels represent a duplication/gene-conversion history rather than the intended orthology history; nonallelic conversion simulations increased PAML false positives, especially with high paralog divergence, long converted tracts, and no outgroup. A universal numerical false-positive rate for “paralog contamination” is not available because it is strongly scenario-dependent. [Casola & Hahn 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19471989/)

- **Misalignment/annotation errors can dominate genome scans.** Simulations found indels themselves innocuous if homologous codons are correctly aligned, but alignment errors produced “unacceptably high” branch-site false positives; PRANK outperformed MUSCLE, MAFFT and ClustalW in that benchmark. In one empirical branch-site scan, manual/score filtering retained only 2,009/3,810 (52.73%) initially called sites—an instability measure, not a calibrated true-positive rate. [Fletcher & Yang 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20447933/) [Zhang et al. 2015](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0126736)

- **Recombination invalidates the single-tree likelihood factorization.** Under neutral simulated recombination, the site-model type-I error reached 90% at high recombination; a related demonstration found false-positive probability rose from <5% toward 100%, exceeding 50% above \(\rho=0.024\) for its HDV-like setting. Detect breakpoints (for example GARD), partition, and fit a tree per partition; this reduces, but does not prove removal of, recombinant history. [Anisimova, Nielsen & Yang 2003](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1462615/) [Delport et al. 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2638624/) [HyPhy](https://hyphy.org/)

- **Multiplicity is a separate error source.** At nominal \(P<0.05\), a 10,000-gene screen has 500 expected null calls before any data/model failure; referee-grade studies state the complete tested family and control FDR (or use a justified hierarchical scheme) across genes, branches, and any post-hoc foreground choices. Selectome explicitly corrects across its full branch-site dataset. [Selectome](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2686563/)

- **Important nonadaptive explanations.** Relaxed constraint and small \(N_e\) permit nearly neutral nonsynonymous substitutions to fix; RELAX was designed because a higher mean \(\omega\) conflates this with intensified positive selection. gBGC favours W→S substitutions, affects neutral flanks and recombination-rich regions, and in one primate analysis could account for >20% of elevated-\(\omega\) cases, including \(\omega>2\). [Wertheim et al. 2015](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/32/3/820/981440) [Eyre-Walker et al. 2002](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/19/12/2142/997518) [Ratnakumar et al. 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2935097/)

- **Composition/model misspecification is demonstrable, not cosmetic.** Standard \(d_N,d_S,\omega\) estimates assume equilibrium composition; simulations and empirical analyses show nonstationarity biases all three, while nonhomogeneous codon models correct the bias. The literature conflicts on ordinary GC variation: one benchmark found branch-site inference robust but underpowered under GC variation/synonymous saturation, whereas nonstationary simulations report false selection signals when frequency and selection shifts interact. [Guéguen & Duret 2018](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/35/3/734/4705835) [Gharib & Robinson-Rechavi 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3684852/) [Baker et al. 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23443835/)

- **Branch-site pre-flight checks.** Demand coding-sequence integrity, protein-guided codon alignment and masking sensitivity, an independently supported gene tree/foreground defined before testing, recombination screening/partitioning, dS/saturation and composition diagnostics, repeated-model convergence, multinucleotide-mutation sensitivity, FDR correction, and inspection of the actual substitutions/sites. Idealized simulations of the modified 2005 test were at or below nominal 5%, but MNMs make codons with multiple differences account for virtually all support in two genome-scale datasets and can yield false positives as often as empirical positives. [Zhang, Nielsen & Yang 2005](https://websites.umich.edu/~zhanglab/publications/2005/Zhang_2005_MBE_22_2472.pdf) [Venkat, Hahn & Thornton 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-018-0584-5)

- **Complements answer different questions.** MK tests contrast nonsynonymous/synonymous polymorphism and divergence; asymptotic MK extrapolates away low-frequency weakly deleterious polymorphisms, while DFE-\(\alpha\) fits the deleterious DFE and demography before estimating adaptive substitutions. Both remain sensitive to demographic change, linkage, reference-site validity and outgroup history. [McDonald & Kreitman 1991](https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1360855567865547904) [Messer & Petrov 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5427504/) [Eyre-Walker & Keightley 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19535738/)

- **Direction of travel.** Polymorphism-aware phylogenetic models jointly model divergence, within-species variation, drift, mutation and selection biases; ML sweep scans (for example diploS/HIC) and ARG+neural approaches (SIA) are increasingly used to classify or estimate selection, but their calibration is conditional on simulated demography, recombination, sampling and error models—not causal proof. [Borges et al. 2022](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/2041-210X.13980) [Kern & Schrider 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5982824/) [Hejase et al. 2021, preprint](https://repository.cshl.edu/id/eprint/40622/)

## What the standard corpus misses

- **Within the last 18 months:** HyPhy’s official materials advertise BUSTED+MSS (global synonymous-selection correction), BUSTED-PH (phenotype-associated/convergent foreground analysis), PRIME, and workflow assets CAPHEINE/AOC in 2026; these deserve scrutiny before routine use because the visited documentation presents them as current capabilities but does not, on that page, provide a peer-reviewed validation paper. [HyPhy news](https://hyphy.org/news/)

- **Version reporting is currently inconsistent across official distribution surfaces:** the GitHub repository listed v2.5.99 as latest (19 May 2026), the HyPhy news page named v2.5.100, and Homebrew listed 2.5.101. A paper should record the exact binary/container hash, analysis script revision, options, genetic code, alignment/tree inputs, and raw JSON—not merely “HyPhy 2.5.” [GitHub](https://github.com/veg/hyphy) [HyPhy news](https://hyphy.org/news/) [Homebrew](https://formulae.brew.sh/formula/hyphy)

- **PoMo/PoMo-like models and ARG-informed population inference are the substantive conceptual shift:** they bring segregating variation, drift and mutation/fixation bias into the model rather than treating one reference per species as a complete history. [Borges et al. 2022](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/2041-210X.13980)

- **ML is becoming an adjunct, not a replacement for model criticism:** diploS/HIC uses simulated training data and SIA remains a 2021 preprint in the source located here; both make simulation coverage, domain shift and calibration central review items. [diploS/HIC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5982824/) [SIA](https://repository.cshl.edu/id/eprint/40622/)

## Caveats and open questions

- There is no defensible universal false-positive percentage for paralogy, misalignment, composition shift, or branch-site tests generally: published numbers depend on divergence, branch lengths, aligner, annotation quality, foreground choice, recombination rate, sequence length, and the null used. Claims beyond the cited simulation setting are **[unverified]**.

- “Synonymous = neutral” is an operational reference assumption, not a biological fact; codon-usage selection, splicing/regulatory constraint, gBGC and mutation-rate variation can perturb it. BUSTED+MSS is relevant precisely because unmodeled synonymous-rate variation can matter, but its broad empirical calibration is **[unverified]** from the sources visited. [HyPhy news](https://hyphy.org/news/)

- Credible sources disagree over how robust the modified branch-site test is to GC variation: the 2013 benchmark found robustness with loss of power under its simulations, whereas nonstationary-model work finds biased \(\omega\) and false selection signals under composition shifts. Treat this as an empirical adequacy question for each dataset, not a settled universal property. [Gharib & Robinson-Rechavi 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3684852/) [Guéguen & Duret 2018](https://academic.oup.com/mbe/article/35/3/734/4705835)

- Functional validation, replicated lineage contrasts, and convergence with polymorphism/DFE evidence strengthen a claim, but none retrospectively rescues an unreported alignment, orthology, tree, recombination, or multiplicity analysis.
