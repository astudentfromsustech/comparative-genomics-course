Regulatory and non-coding comparison

King & Wilson's 1975 observation was that humans and chimpanzees have unexpectedly similar proteins despite marked organismal differences. It motivated regulation as a candidate explanatory layer — it did not demonstrate one.

The answer, first

Conservation is not the same object as function

What a conserved non-coding element actually is

Operationally: a non-coding aligned sequence evolving more slowly than an estimated neutral model. That is the whole definition.

It is not a synonym for enhancer. Bona fide enhancers can be weakly conserved, transposable-element-derived, or lineage-specific — so the CNE set both misses real enhancers and includes constrained sequence that is not one.

2
Turnover

The Villar result, stated precisely

Roughly half — but of what?

Villar et al. mapped H3K27ac and H3K4me3 in liver across 20 mammals and reported that roughly half of active enhancers per species were lineage- or species-specific, while promoters were more conserved.

This is a tissue-, mark- and orthology-dependent rate — not a genome-wide enhancer-turnover constant. It is very often cited as though it were the latter.

And a revision that changes how you read reporter assays

Human accelerated region experiments show that compensatory substitutions can mask substantial nucleotide-level effects behind little net reporter difference. A null result in a reporter assay is therefore weak evidence that the sequence differences do nothing.

3
Expression

Why a cross-species expression difference is usually uninterpretable

Bulk cross-species RNA-seq requires reciprocal, orthology-aware annotation; species-balanced biological replication; matched protocols; and an explicit design for species, batch, individual, sex, stage and cell composition.

annotation asymmetry
Gene-length and annotation differences can create false differential expression outright — Chapter 04 arriving in Chapter 14
composition
A tissue is a mixture. A "difference in expression" is often a difference in what cells the tissue contains
batch ≡ species
If each species was processed as a batch, species and batch are perfectly confounded and no normalisation recovers the difference
stage matching
See below — the one people are most confident about and most wrong about

Chronological age is not stage matching

Comparative mammalian transcriptomes infer stage correspondences from expression and reveal substantial heterochrony — a newborn opossum corresponds approximately to mouse E11.5.

Sampling "newborns" across mammals is therefore not a matched design. It is a design that measures developmental timing differences and reports them as regulatory divergence.

The elegant control: hybrid cells

Human–chimpanzee hybrid iPSC derivatives put both genomes in a shared trans environment. Allele-specific expression and accessibility then estimate cis divergence while avoiding composition, stage, culture and batch confounders at once.

What those studies found matters: substantial contributions from both cis and trans. So "regulatory evolution" must not be silently equated with cis-only evolution — a conflation that is common precisely because cis is what is easiest to measure.

4
Single cell

Cell-type homology is contested, not solved

Atlases support deep conservation of broad cell categories and core regulatory programs, while also identifying lineage-specific states and conserved programs deployed in new contexts.

One-to-one orthologues only

loses signal

Restricting the feature space to strict orthologues discards species-specific genes — often the ones the study is about.

Permissive mapping + integration

overcorrects

Permissive homology mappings and integration methods can overcorrect real species differences, removing the biology as though it were batch effect.

The benchmark result

A comparison of 28 gene-homology and integration combinations found the outcome explicitly method- and task-dependent. There is no default that is safe across questions — so the mapping choice has to be justified against the specific question and its sensitivity reported.

What moved in the last 18 months

The Biodiversity Cell Atlas (2025) formalised a large cross-taxa atlas initiative; human-specific-deletion work used hybrid cells plus reporter assays to assign cis/trans targets; and a 2025 mammal–bird study added comparative enhancer assays of accelerated regions.

These are advances in resources and candidate validation — not a resolution of phenotype-level causality. King & Wilson's question remains open.

Acceptance test

You have understood Chapter 14 when…

Five things, without notes

  1. Define a CNE operationally, and say why it is not an enhancer.
  2. State the Villar result with its scope conditions.
  3. Name four confounders that make a cross-species expression difference uninterpretable.
  4. Explain why hybrid cells are an unusually clean design, and what they showed about cis versus trans.
  5. Say what MPRA, CRISPRi and an allele swap each do and do not establish.
Self-check

A paper reports 412 genes differentially expressed between human and macaque prefrontal cortex, enrichment for synaptic genes, overlap with human accelerated regions, and concludes regulatory rewiring underlying human cognitive evolution. What do you ask?

composition
Cortex is a cell mixture, and neuron/glia ratios differ between species. Was composition estimated and adjusted, or is this a cell-count difference reported as expression?
stage & state
Matched how? Chronological age is not stage matching, and post-mortem interval, agonal state and tissue handling differ systematically between human and macaque samples.
batch ≡ species
Human and macaque tissue almost never come from the same pipeline. If species and batch are confounded, no normalisation separates them.
annotation
Reciprocal, orthology-aware, uniformly re-annotated? Gene-length differences alone create false DE.
cis or trans?
Nothing here distinguishes them. Hybrid cells would; expression comparison alone cannot.
the HAR overlap
How was significance of overlap computed, against what background? And note the compensatory-substitution result: HAR reporter effects can be masked, so overlap direction is not interpretable without functional work.

The deepest problem is the last clause. "Underlying human cognitive evolution" is a rung-four claim supported by rung-one evidence, in a system where the causal experiment is not available — which is exactly why this chapter's question has stayed open for fifty years.
