Convergence

The strictest application of the evidence ladder in the field — and the one with the best-documented walk-backs. Two famous convergence claims were substantially overturned, both instructively.

Question
What does it take to claim convergent adaptation rather than coincidence?
Prerequisite
Chapters 08, 09, 11, 12
Corpus
F5/convergence 2/4
Understood when
You can count independent origins, and explain hemiplasy
1
The answer, first

Three conditions, all required

Finding the same phenotype in multiple taxa is not enough. Neither is finding the same substitution.

1 · independent origins
The phenotype has multiple genuinely independent origins under a defensible trait-evolution reconstruction
2 · exceeds a real null
The molecular pattern exceeds a null incorporating branch length, mutation and compositional bias, site constraint, phylogenetic uncertainty and gene-tree discordance
3 · specifically aligned
The association tracks the phenotype rather than a correlated ecological variable, shared ancestry, relaxed constraint, or annotation bias

Chance convergence is not rare

It rises with total divergence, amino-acid accessibility, site constraint and reconstruction error. So a convergent-substitution count must be compared to an explicit substitution-model expectation — and divergent substitutions, or matched branches, make useful empirical controls.

2
The trap

Hemiplasy — one mutation that looks like two

The required diagnostic

Analyse local genealogies and recombination blocks, use coalescent-aware methods, and treat gene-tree/species-tree concordance as a required check rather than an optional one. PhyloAcc-GT (2023) integrates gene-tree uncertainty under the multispecies coalescent specifically to reduce ILS-driven false calls.

3
Counting

Trait-positive tips are not independent origins

This is Chapter 09's lesson in its sharpest form. Origins must be counted as posterior-distributed transitions, not tip labels.

how to count origins defensibly

  fit alternative discrete-trait models
  on a DATED SPECIES-TREE POSTERIOR          not one point-estimate tree
  stochastic-map the transitions
  propagate uncertainty into the genomic test

report  the DISTRIBUTION of origins,  and effective independent contrasts
       — not "we studied 14 species with the trait"

Standard ancestral reconstructions can be biased by rate heterogeneity and
by state-dependent diversification. Closely related trait-positive tips are
replication-poor; treating them as independent inflates significance.
4
Case one

Echolocating mammals — what actually happened

The claim, 2013

Nature

Nearly 200 convergent loci across 2,326 proteins in echolocating mammals — a genome-wide shared coding basis for echolocation.

The rebuttals, 2015

no matched null

The tree-distortion signal had no appropriate matched null. Equivalent "convergence" appeared for echolocators versus non-echolocating controls, and the selection test was misapplied.

The genome-wide claim did not survive.

But read the ending carefully — this is a walk-back, not a retraction

The original article remains published. A small set of auditory candidates remained plausible, and later work using a convergence-versus-divergence enrichment screen recovered a narrower functional enrichment in cochlear-ganglion-associated genes.

So the credible disagreement now is about localised functional signal — not about the original "widespread genome-wide convergence" conclusion, which is gone. Getting this distinction right is itself a test of whether you have read the literature or the headline.

5
Case two

High-altitude waterfowl — the more damaging lesson

Parallel substitutions were largely the wrong ones

Claims that parallel haemoglobin substitutions explained repeated high-affinity haemoglobin were weakened by protein engineering and direct functional measurement:

most parallel substitutions
were irrelevant to oxygen affinity
most functional convergence
came from non-parallel substitutions

This is more damaging than the echolocation case, because the statistics were not obviously wrong — the parallel substitutions were real. Molecular parallelism and functional convergence were simply different sets. No amount of better null modelling would have found that; only functional measurement did.

The generalisable lesson

Convergence detected at the sequence level is a hypothesis about function that sequence cannot test. If the claim is adaptive convergence, the parallel sites are the candidates — and the functional work is the evidence.

The ladder

Four rungs, and the wording each buys

RungRequiresYou may write
CandidateAn outlier, or observed homoplasy"convergent substitutions were observed at…"
Controlled associationTrait-linked excess after calibrated nulls and multiplicity control"…is associated with convergence in this trait"
Mechanistic supportThe allele or element reproducibly changes the relevant molecular, cellular or organismal function"…alters function in the expected direction"
Causal adaptiveAncestral/derived swaps or precise editing — ideally reciprocal rescue — plus fitness or performance measurement in the relevant environment"…is a cause of the convergent adaptation"

Status of this ladder

It is an evidential standard, not a formal journal rule. But high-tier papers increasingly require the top two rungs before wording a hit as causal adaptation rather than as associated with convergence — and the waterfowl case is why.

  2. Explain hemiplasy, and why it bites hardest exactly where convergence is studied.
  3. Say how independent origins should be counted and reported.
  4. State what the echolocation rebuttals actually established — and what survived.
  5. Explain why the waterfowl case could not have been caught by better statistics.
Self-check

A paper reports that across 9 independently evolved cave fish populations, 23 genes show convergent amino-acid substitutions, enriched for eye development, concluding convergent molecular basis for eye loss. What do you require?

are they independent?
"Nine populations" of one species in one cave system may be one colonisation. Give the posterior distribution of origins from stochastic mapping on a dated tree posterior — not a count of populations.
hemiplasy
Recently diverged cave populations are the maximum-risk case: high ILS, standing variation, possible gene flow. A single ancestral standing variant sorted differently would look exactly like this. Local genealogies, please.
matched null
Run the same pipeline on surface populations, or on a phenotype-shuffled control. That is precisely what the echolocation rebuttals did, and it is what changed the answer.
relaxed constraint
Eye loss is the textbook case of relaxed constraint, not positive selection. Convergent substitutions in eye genes are the expected consequence of released purifying selection. Run RELAX.
the enrichment
Post-hoc functional annotation of a statistical result, on a phenotype whose gene set was known in advance. Weak evidence.
function
The waterfowl lesson: are these 23 the substitutions that actually change eye development? Nothing so far tests that.

Cave fish are a genuinely strong system for repeated evolution. That is exactly why the controls matter — a system where the answer is probably yes is where a weak test is least likely to be challenged.
